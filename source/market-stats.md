# Phuket market stats

Source: https://phukettravels.com/stats (Thailand Ministry of Tourism and Sports provincial statistics 2012-2025, updated Sept 2026). Fetched 2026-10-08.

## 2025
- Total visitors: 14,248,853 (tourists + same-day visitors)
- Foreign visitors: 10,748,204 (75.4%)
- Foreign guest arrivals (accommodation check-ins): 10,085,407
- Hotel occupancy: 76.3%
- Average daily spend: 8,563 THB (~$255)
- Tourism revenue: 555,428 million THB (~$16.5B)
- Latest month July 2026: 1,135,382 visitors, 74.2% occupancy (preliminary)
